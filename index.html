Tracking

The model labels limbs anatomically and can exchange left for right partway through a clip. Corrected runs are flagged in amber on the timeline.

Body tracking gives four coarse points per hand. Finger tracking adds a second model — more detail, roughly half the frame rate.

Crop & rescan

Draw a box on the active clip, then either crop the view to it or re-run detection inside it.

Rescan covers the box in space and the trimmed range in time, and replaces only those frames.

It helps most when the subject is too small or too dim for the full frame to find at all, or when the wrong person is picked. If the subject is already tracked, expect little change — the model already crops to whoever it detected. For finer elbows and wrists, the heavy model is the bigger lever.

Focus scan

Watches the clip from the playhead, then locks onto whichever of the chosen points it can actually see — and tracks only those.
